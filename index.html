I am an Assistant Professor of computer science in the Département Science et Technologie at Université TÉLUQ, Montréal. My research focuses on the design and evaluation of ultrasound-guided navigation systems for neurosurgery, and on the development of new smart-sensor and immersive-visualization technologies applied to medicine.

My work covers three research axes:

  • computer-assisted surgery;
  • surgical education and simulation;
  • design of low-cost medical systems.

Fields of expertise

  • Medical imaging
  • Machine learning
  • Virtual and augmented reality
  • Digital image processing
  • Simulation and surgical applications

Education

  • Postdoctoral fellowship in computer-assisted surgery, McGill University
  • Ph.D. in engineering, École de technologie supérieure (ÉTS), 2017
  • M.Sc. in computer science (image processing), Université Paris Descartes
  • B.Sc. in software engineering, Université des Sciences et de la Technologie Houari Boumediene (USTHB), Algiers
