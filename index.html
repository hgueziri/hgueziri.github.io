I am an Assistant Professor (professeur adjoint) of computer science in the Département Science et Technologie at Université TÉLUQ, Montréal. My research focuses on the design and evaluation of ultrasound-guided navigation systems for neurosurgery, and on the development of new smart-sensor and immersive-visualization technologies applied to medicine.

My work covers three research axes:

  • computer-assisted surgery;
  • surgical education and simulation;
  • design of low-cost medical systems.

From 2017, I worked as a postdoctoral fellow and then as a research associate in the Department of Neurology and Neurosurgery at McGill University, in the Neuroimaging and Surgical Technologies lab of the Montreal Neurological Institute. There, I developed image-guided surgery software for brain and spine interventions, with a particular focus on ultrasound-CT/MR image registration, interactive image segmentation, surface and volume reconstruction, and augmented ultrasound visualization.

I received my Ph.D. in engineering, specialized in health technologies, from École de technologie supérieure (ÉTS) in 2017, where I worked on the user-centered design and evaluation of interactive segmentation methods for medical images. I am also a member of the Ordre des ingénieurs du Québec.

Fields of expertise

  • Medical imaging
  • Machine learning
  • Virtual and augmented reality
  • Digital image processing
  • Simulation and surgical applications
